Privacy Policy

Blendy Toons Bot. Last updated 8 November 2026.

Who uses this app

Blendy Toons Bot is a private tool. Only the owner of the Blendy Toons channel uses it, on their own computer, to upload videos to their own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

What data it uses

When the owner connects their account, the app receives access permission from Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook). It uses this only to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles, descriptions, covers and publish times, add them to the channel's own playlists, and read the channel's own view counts and statistics.

Where data is kept

Access permissions are stored encrypted, only on the owner's computer. The app has no server and does not send account data anywhere except to Google and Meta, to do the tasks above.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the app's Accounts page, from Google account permissions, or from Facebook's Business Integrations settings.
